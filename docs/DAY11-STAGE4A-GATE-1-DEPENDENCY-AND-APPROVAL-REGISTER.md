## 1. Executive Summary & Gate 1 Authority

Governance Gate 1 represents the mandatory legal, regulatory, and financial control boundary separating **Read-Only Technical Assessment & Design (Stages 1–4A)** from **Authorized Software Implementation & Financial Execution (Stage 4B+)**.

### Strict Governance Separation & Inviolable Principles:
1. **Technical Design Does Not Equal Governance Approval:** The completion of technical implementation blueprints, schema designs, or test strategies in Stage 4A **does NOT satisfy** any human stakeholder prerequisite.
2. **Five Distinct Dimensions Distinguished:**
   - *Technical Deliverable Completion:* Design specifications and gap audits are 100% complete.
   - *External Evidence Availability:* `EXTERNAL EVIDENCE NOT AVAILABLE IN THE INSPECTED PROJECT REPOSITORY — INDEPENDENT EXTERNAL VALIDATION PENDING.`
   - *Governance Review Completion:* Internal documentation audits have been conducted.
   - *Formal Stakeholder Approval:* Executive voting on Policies A–E is pending (0 of 18 decisions approved).
   - *Gate 1 Authorization:* Unlocking protocol requires all 14 active blocking conditions to clear.
3. **No Automatic Promotion:** A prerequisite cannot be promoted to satisfied merely because a technical design document exists.
4. **Zero Engineering Authority to Unlock:** Neither engineering leads, technical auditors, nor automated agents possess the authority to unlock Gate 1.
5. **Mandatory Multi-Party Executive Unlocking:** Gate 1 can only be unlocked by formal, unanimous, verified sign-offs from four designated corporate officers:
   - Chief Financial Officer (`EXEC-CFO`)
   - Corporate Controller (`FIN-CTRL`)
   - Vice President of Human Resources (`HR-VPHR`)
   - Head of Internal Audit (`AUD-HEAD`)
