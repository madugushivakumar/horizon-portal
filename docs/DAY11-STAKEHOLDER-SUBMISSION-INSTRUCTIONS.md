## 1. Purpose & Scope of This Guide

This operational protocol instructs the **human project owner** on how to safely, professionally, and accurately submit the formal Day 11 Policy Approval Requests (Policies A through E) to the designated corporate executive stakeholders.

### Mandatory Operational Boundaries:
- **Zero Automated Dispatch:** The agentic system does not automatically send emails, dispatch tickets, or trigger external network calls. All external transmissions must be executed by authorized human personnel.
- **Multichannel Flexibility:** Email is **not** the only permitted submission channel. Project owners may utilize any sanctioned corporate transmission mechanism:
  1. *Corporate Email Dispatch* (utilizing prepared drafts in `docs/DAY11-EMAIL-POLICY-*.md`).
  2. *Internal Compliance & Governance Systems* (e.g., ServiceNow Governance, Jira Compliance, Workiva, Archer).
  3. *Executive PDF Memos with Digital Signatures* (DocuSign, Adobe Sign, PKI-backed signatures).
  4. *Physical Hardcopy Transmittal* with ink signatures and formal corporate seal.
  5. *Formal Board / Audit Committee Resolutions*.

## 4. Evidence Retention & Audit Packaging Instructions

When a written decision is delivered by an executive stakeholder, the project owner must adhere strictly to the following evidence retention rules:

1. **Non-Repudiation Invariant:** Never convert verbal agreements, phone calls, slack messages, or informal comments into an approval. Only formal written, attributable records are valid.
2. **Evidence Preservation:**
   - For email responses: Save the full raw RFC 822 email file (`.eml` / `.msg`) including all mail server transit headers.
   - For PDF documents: Ensure digital cryptographic certificate metadata is preserved intact.
   - For paper memos: Scan the document at 300+ DPI in color, ensuring wet signatures and corporate stamps are fully legible.
3. **Registering the Evidence:**
   - Update [`docs/DAY11-STAKEHOLDER-RESPONSE-REGISTER.md`](file:///c:/Users/Madugu%20Shiva%20Kumar/OneDrive/图片/Desktop/horizon-portal/docs/DAY11-STAKEHOLDER-RESPONSE-REGISTER.md) with exact timestamp, approver metadata, conditions, and evidence reference.
   - Update [`docs/DAY11-STAKEHOLDER-APPROVAL-TRACKER.md`](file:///c:/Users/Madugu%20Shiva%20Kumar/OneDrive/图片/Desktop/horizon-portal/docs/DAY11-STAKEHOLDER-APPROVAL-TRACKER.md) (`Sent: Yes`, `Response Received: Yes`, `Verified: Yes`).
   - Update [`docs/DAY11-STEP2B-POLICY-APPROVAL-RECORD.md`](file:///c:/Users/Madugu%20Shiva%20Kumar/OneDrive/图片/Desktop/horizon-portal/docs/DAY11-STEP2B-POLICY-APPROVAL-RECORD.md).
4. **Permanent Archiving:** Store all received evidence artifacts in the designated project audit repository (`docs/evidence/` or enterprise compliance archive).
