## 1. Register Overview & Governance Principles

This master register functions as the definitive source of truth tracking the lifecycle of human stakeholder approvals across all five Day 11 governance policies (Policies A, B, C, D, and E).

### Core Audit Rules:
1. **Separation of Stages:**
   - *Stage 1 (Prepared):* Approval packages, evidence summaries, and voting options drafted $\implies$ **COMPLETE (5 / 5)**
   - *Stage 2 (Submitted):* Requests dispatched to verified executive email endpoints $\implies$ **BLOCKED — HUMAN DISPATCH REQUIRED (0 / 5)**
   - *Stage 3 (Response Received):* Stakeholder responses returned $\implies$ **NOT RECEIVED (0 / 5)**
   - *Stage 4 (Identity & Authority Verified):* Approver identity and role authority verified $\implies$ **PENDING VERIFICATION (0 / 5)**
   - *Stage 5 (Decision Verified):* Explicit written decision verified against source standard $\implies$ **PENDING APPROVAL (0 / 5)**
   - *Stage 6 (Formally Recorded):* Decisions codified in official register with audit hash $\implies$ **PENDING (0 / 5)**
   - *Stage 7 (Gate 1 Reviewed):* Gate 1 review convened by executive committee $\implies$ **GATE 1 BLOCKED (0 / 5)**
2. **Zero False Approvals:** No policy may be marked `APPROVED` without verifiable written documentation (e.g., signed PDF, authenticated email headers, board resolution).
3. **Execution Separation:** Policy approval does not authorize financial execution; execution requires separate operational sign-off (Key 2).
