### Clarification on Policy Approval vs. Execution Authorization:
1. **Key 1 (Policy Approval):** Adopts the accounting policy, defines permissible classifications, and bounds the legal framework.
2. **Key 2 (Execution Authorization):** Authorizes a specific operational user to apply a specific numerical adjustment to a specific database record within an audited transaction.
3. **Core Mandate:** Even if all five policies were unanimously approved today, **Gate 1 would remain LOCKED until a separate Key 2 execution voucher is prepared, submitted, and approved by an independent finance executive**.
