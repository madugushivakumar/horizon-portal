## 1. Decision Lifecycle Framework & Permitted Statuses

Under corporate governance standards, a decision cannot be recognized as approved based on informal discussions, technical assumptions, or unratified documents. 

### Authorized Decision Status Classifications:
- `PROPOSED — APPROVAL PENDING`: Formal technical recommendation formulated; awaiting corporate stakeholder submission and action. Baseline state for all 18 decisions.
- `SUBMITTED FOR REVIEW`: Formal transmission pack delivered to the authorized corporate stakeholder; receipt confirmed.
- `UNDER DELIBERATION`: Stakeholder group has formally convened to evaluate options, evidence, and risk trade-offs.
- `APPROVED`: Authorized decision-maker has executed a binding approval record (Template 1) supported by verified corporate authority.
- `APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS`: Approved subject to specific operational, technical, or financial conditions precedents.
- `REJECTED`: Decision rejected by authorized leadership; alternative policy formulation required.
- `DEFERRED`: Stakeholder group formally postponed determination pending future milestones.
- `MORE EVIDENCE REQUIRED`: Deliberation halted due to insufficient supporting evidence or documentation.
