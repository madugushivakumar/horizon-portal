## 1. Objective and Authorized Response Statuses

This register provides the formal, controlled intake logging mechanism for recording responses, determination records, and authorizations received from corporate stakeholders regarding the 18 Day 11 governance decisions and 14 external evidence requirements.

### Permitted Response Status Lifecycle:
- `NOT_RECEIVED`: Initial baseline state. No formal response document has been received by the repository team.
- `RECEIVED — AUTHENTICITY PENDING`: A physical or digital response record has been deposited; source authentication is under review.
- `AUTHENTICITY VERIFIED`: The document has been cryptographically, digitally, or formally verified as originating from the designated stakeholder.
- `AUTHORITY VERIFICATION PENDING`: Authenticity is established, but formal corporate authority (delegation of authority, corporate charter, board resolution) is being verified.
- `ACCEPTED FOR GOVERNANCE REVIEW`: Document authenticity and legal/corporate authority are validated; contents are under technical/audit review.
- `REJECTED — INSUFFICIENT AUTHORITY OR EVIDENCE`: Submitted response rejected due to lack of corporate authority, signature deficiency, or evidentiary failure.
- `CLARIFICATION REQUIRED`: Response is ambiguous, contradictory, or contains conditional stipulations requiring further consultation.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Governance Principle:** A response document being received does **not** constitute an approved decision. An approval can only be recognized when authenticity, authority, and prerequisite satisfaction are verified and formally recorded.
