## 3. Standard Operating Procedure: Approval Evidence Verification

When formal written approval is subsequently supplied by a stakeholder, the verification auditor must execute the following 10-step protocol:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    APPROVAL EVIDENCE VERIFICATION PROTOCOL                      │
└────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                         │
    ┌────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────┐
    ▼                                    ▼                                    ▼
1. Match Policy ID              2. Verify Approver Role              3. Inspect Decision
   (A, B, C, D, or E)              (Designation Authority)              (APPROVED / CONDITIONAL)
    │                                    │                                    │
    ▼                                    ▼                                    ▼
4. Verify Sign Date             5. Log Conditions / Notes            6. Validate Evidence Ref
   (Calendar valid)                (Audit constraints)                  (Memo / Resolution ID)
    │                                    │                                    │
    ▼                                    ▼                                    ▼
7. Check Conflicts              8. Update Governance Ledger          9. Update Tracker Status
   (Cross-policy harmony)          (STEP2B-POLICY-APPROVAL-RECORD)      (STAKEHOLDER-APPROVAL-TRACKER)
    │
    ▼
10. Archive Evidence Artifact
    (Preserve permanent audit copy)
```

### Detailed Verification Steps:
1. **Match Policy ID:** Confirm the submission unambiguously maps to Policy A, B, C, D, or E.
2. **Verify Approver Designation:** Ensure the individual holding the title possesses signing authority for the designated policy domain.
3. **Verify Decision:** Confirm selection of `APPROVED` or `APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS`. If marked `REJECTED` or `NEEDS REVISION`, immediately pause and prepare a formal architectural revision notice.
4. **Verify Date:** Verify the timestamp and signature date.
5. **Verify Conditions:** Document all caveats, restrictions, or operational conditions.
6. **Verify Supporting Evidence:** Confirm presence of a valid internal memo ID, board resolution number, or cryptographic signature.
7. **Identify Conflicting Instructions:** Confirm that the decision does not contradict another stakeholder's policy determination.
8. **Update Governance Ledger:** Update [`docs/DAY11-STEP2B-POLICY-APPROVAL-RECORD.md`](file:///c:/Users/Madugu%20Shiva%20Kumar/OneDrive/图片/Desktop/horizon-portal/docs/DAY11-STEP2B-POLICY-APPROVAL-RECORD.md).
9. **Update Stakeholder Tracker:** Update this tracker (`Sent: Yes`, `Response Received: Yes`, `Verified: Yes`, `Status: Approved`).
10. **Preserve Original Evidence:** Archive physical/electronic evidence files permanently in project audit storage.

## 4. Rejection or Revision Protocol

If any policy is rejected or modified by an executive approver:
- **Zero Architecture Changes Without Review:** The engineering team will **not** silently alter backend code, schemas, or models.
- **Formal Revision Proposal:** The team will draft a formal revised policy proposal detailing the trade-offs, statutory risks, and architectural adjustments for stakeholder re-review.
