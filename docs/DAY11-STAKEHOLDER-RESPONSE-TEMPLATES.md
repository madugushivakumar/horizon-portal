## 1. Executive Summary & Template Usage Directives

This document establishes the official **Blank Stakeholder Response Templates** to be distributed to authorized corporate stakeholders during formal review sessions.

### Mandatory Governance Rules:
1. **Never Populate on Behalf of Stakeholders:** Technical teams, AI assistants, and system auditors are strictly prohibited from pre-populating, simulating, or forging stakeholder determinations, names, or signatures.
2. **Standardized Response Categories:** Every determination must select one of six authorized response codes.
3. **Quorum Identification Mandate:** A dedicated template is provided for corporate leadership to declare the authoritative governance instrument governing voting thresholds.
