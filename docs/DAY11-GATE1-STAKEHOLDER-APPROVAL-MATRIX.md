## 1. Executive Summary & Governance Integrity Rules

This document establishes the formal Stakeholder Approval Matrix for Gate 1 of the Day 11 Payroll Reconciliation initiative.

Under enterprise governance standards, technical readiness must be strictly separated from corporate stakeholder authority. While engineering teams have designed models, gap analyses, and test frameworks, **zero authority exists for engineering to approve financial policies, monetary thresholds, or legal compliance frameworks**.

### Inviolable Governance Principles:
1. **Zero Fabrication:** No stakeholder names, personal email addresses, digital signatures, or meeting minutes are invented or assumed.
2. **No Role Substitution:** An engineer's or architect's technical concurrence cannot substitute for a Chief Financial Officer's or Corporate Controller's financial authorization.
3. **Strict Remit Segregation:** No stakeholder may approve an area outside their documented corporate responsibility (e.g., HR cannot approve General Ledger accounting policies; Finance cannot approve employment-status taxonomies).
4. **Unestablished Authority Classification:** Wherever the decision-maker is not formally ratified in approved corporate bylaws, the authority is designated:
   $$\mathbf{AUTHORITY\ NOT\ YET\ APPROVED\ —\ GOVERNANCE\ DECISION\ REQUIRED}$$
