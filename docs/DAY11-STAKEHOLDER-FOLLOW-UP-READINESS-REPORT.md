## 6. Approval Evidence Requirements

When written responses are returned, the auditor must strictly verify that all 7 mandatory evidence criteria are met before updating any status:

- [ ] **Documented Stakeholder Attribution:** The decision must clearly identify the individual holding the designated corporate title.
- [ ] **Explicit Decision Code:** Must explicitly state `APPROVED` or `APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS`. Unsigned drafts or ambiguous comments are rejected.
- [ ] **Calendar Timestamp:** Verifiable signing date must be present.
- [ ] **Traceable Evidence Identifier:** Must reference an internal memo ID, board resolution number, signed PDF file, or authenticated email header.
- [ ] **Cross-Policy Harmony:** Directives must not contradict other policies (e.g. Policy A payslip calculation vs Policy C historical immutability).
- [ ] **Verbatim Condition Recording:** Every condition or directive attached to an approval must be transcribed into the register and assessed against technical implementation plans.
- [ ] **Non-Repudiation Storage:** Original email files (`.eml` / `.msg`), signed PDFs, or scan files must be permanently preserved in the project audit archive.
